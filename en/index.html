About

Active as waniwave since around 2012. As a rapper and track maker, waniwave has put out several releases and composed music for independent doujin games. In Ghostleg, a duo with Niimariko, waniwave writes the lyrics and music and handles all of the programming.

Since 2018 also a writer, contributing to Game*Spark under the pen name Bunsho Kakuhiko, with bylines in Nlab, KAI-YOU, Dengeki Online and others. Host and scriptwriter of the web radio show “Game*Spark no Radio Zen-i X.”

Lately, a lifework: posting a growing collection of short stories co-written with AI on note (in Japanese).
